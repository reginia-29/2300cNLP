    How it was trained

    Skip-gram with negative sampling (gensim) on text8, a cleaned 100 MB slice of English Wikipedia. The model learns to predict the words around each word, so words used in similar contexts end up with similar vectors.

    How "closest" works

    Every word is a vector. Closeness is cosine similarity: the cosine of the angle between two vectors, from −1 to 1. The page compares your word against every word in the vocabulary, right in your browser, and keeps the top ten.

    How the 3D view works

    PCA finds the three directions along which the shown words differ most and projects them onto those axes. It's a squashed view of 100 dimensions, so the percent of variance kept tells you how faithful the picture is.
