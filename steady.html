Daily mental wellness check-in

Notice how you're really doing.

A two-minute check-in that turns vague feelings into patterns you can see. Track your mood, energy, and what's going on, then spot what helps and what drains you.

Start today's check-in See an example

How it works

1

Check in

Pick a mood from Rough to Great and slide your energy and stress. That's the whole required part.

2

Add context

Tap what shaped your day, like sleep, exercise, or work stress. Add a note if you want to.

3

See your patterns

After a week or two, Steady shows trends and which tags line up with better or harder days.

Private by design

  • Your check-ins stay in this browser in this prototype.
  • No ads, and no selling or sharing your data.
  • Copy or delete everything at any time.

Gentle, not pushy

  • No streaks to lose and no guilt for missed days.
  • Insights are observations from your own entries.
  • Support resources are always one tap away.

Steady is a self-reflection tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or replace care from a licensed professional. If you are in crisis, find help now.
